## Question

A current of 5 A is passed for 32 minutes 10 seconds through aqueous copper(II) tetraoxosulphate(VI) using inert electrodes. Calculate the mass of copper deposited at the cathode. The cathode reaction is

Cu2+ + 2e- -> Cu

[Cu = 64; 1 faraday = 96500 C/mol]

## Solution

1. First find the quantity of electricity, Q = It, with the time in seconds.
2. Time = (32 x 60) + 10 = 1930 s.
3. Q = 5 x 1930 = 9650 C.
4. One faraday, 96500 C, is the charge on one mole of electrons, so divide by it to get the moles of electrons passed.
5. Moles of electrons = 9650 / 96500 = 0.1 mol.
6. The cathode equation shows that 2 moles of electrons deposit 1 mole of copper, because Cu2+ is doubly charged.
7. Moles of Cu = 0.1 / 2 = 0.05 mol.
8. Mass of Cu = 0.05 x 64 = 3.2 g.
9. Copper is deposited rather than hydrogen because Cu2+ is below H+ in the discharge series and so is discharged in preference to it.

**Answer: 3.2 g of copper**
