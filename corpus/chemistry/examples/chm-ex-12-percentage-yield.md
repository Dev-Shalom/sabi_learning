## Question

50 g of pure calcium trioxocarbonate(IV) is heated and 21 g of calcium oxide is obtained. The equation is

CaCO3 -> CaO + CO2

Calculate (a) the theoretical yield of calcium oxide, (b) the percentage yield. [C = 12, O = 16, Ca = 40]

## Solution

1. Part (a). The theoretical yield is what the equation says should be obtained if the reaction went to completion with no losses.
2. Molar mass of CaCO3 = 40 + 12 + (3 x 16) = 100 g/mol.
3. Moles of CaCO3 = 50 / 100 = 0.5 mol.
4. From the equation, 1 mole of CaCO3 gives 1 mole of CaO, so 0.5 mol of CaO should form.
5. Molar mass of CaO = 40 + 16 = 56 g/mol, so the theoretical yield = 0.5 x 56 = 28 g.
6. Part (b). Percentage yield = (actual yield / theoretical yield) x 100.
7. Percentage yield = (21 / 28) x 100 = 75 per cent.
8. It is below 100 per cent because the decomposition may be incomplete and because solid is lost in transferring it from the crucible.

**Answer: (a) 28 g  (b) 75 per cent**
