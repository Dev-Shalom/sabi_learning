## Question

12 g of magnesium is burnt completely in oxygen. Calculate the mass of magnesium oxide formed. The equation is

2Mg + O2 -> 2MgO

[O = 16, Mg = 24]

## Solution

1. A balanced equation is a statement about moles, so convert the mass given into moles first.
2. Moles of Mg = 12 / 24 = 0.5 mol.
3. From the equation, 2 moles of Mg give 2 moles of MgO, a ratio of 1 to 1.
4. So moles of MgO formed = 0.5 mol.
5. Molar mass of MgO = 24 + 16 = 40 g/mol.
6. Mass of MgO = 0.5 x 40 = 20 g.
7. The product is heavier than the magnesium because the 8 g difference is the oxygen that combined with it. Mass is conserved overall.

**Answer: 20 g of magnesium oxide**
