## Question

50 cm3 of 0.8 mol/dm3 hydrochloric acid is diluted with distilled water to 200 cm3. Calculate the concentration of the diluted solution.

## Solution

1. Adding water adds no solute, so the number of moles of HCl is the same before and after. That gives C1V1 = C2V2.
2. Check with the moles. Before: 0.8 x (50 / 1000) = 0.04 mol.
3. Rearranging: C2 = C1V1 / V2. Both volumes are in cm3, so they may be used as they stand provided they are in the same unit.
4. C2 = (0.8 x 50) / 200 = 40 / 200.
5. C2 = 0.2 mol/dm3.
6. The volume was multiplied by 4, so the concentration has fallen by the same factor: 0.8 / 4 = 0.2.

**Answer: 0.2 mol/dm3**
