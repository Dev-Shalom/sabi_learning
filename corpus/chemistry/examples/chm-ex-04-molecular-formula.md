## Question

A hydrocarbon has the empirical formula CH2 and a relative molecular mass of 56. Determine its molecular formula. [H = 1, C = 12]

## Solution

1. The molecular formula is always a whole-number multiple of the empirical formula, so first find the mass of one empirical unit.
2. Empirical formula mass of CH2 = 12 + (2 x 1) = 14.
3. Divide the relative molecular mass by the empirical formula mass to find the multiple.
4. Multiple = 56 / 14 = 4.
5. Multiply every subscript in CH2 by 4.
6. Molecular formula = C4H8.
7. Check it. C4H8 has a relative molecular mass of (4 x 12) + (8 x 1) = 56, as given. It fits CnH2n, so it is an alkene.

**Answer: C4H8**
