## Question

(a) An alkane has a relative molecular mass of 58. (b) An alkyne has a relative molecular mass of 40. Determine the molecular formula and the name of each. [H = 1, C = 12]

## Solution

1. Start from the general formula of the series and write its relative molecular mass in terms of n.
2. Part (a). Alkanes are CnH2n+2, so the mass is (12n) + (1 x (2n + 2)) = 12n + 2n + 2 = 14n + 2.
3. 14n + 2 = 58, so 14n = 56 and n = 4.
4. With n = 4 the formula is C4H10, which is butane.
5. Part (b). Alkynes are CnH2n-2, so the mass is 12n + 2n - 2 = 14n - 2.
6. 14n - 2 = 40, so 14n = 42 and n = 3.
7. With n = 3 the formula is C3H4, which is propyne.
8. Check both. C4H10: (4 x 12) + (10 x 1) = 58. C3H4: (3 x 12) + (4 x 1) = 40.
9. Members of one homologous series differ by CH2, which is 14 units of mass. That is why 14 keeps appearing.

**Answer: (a) C4H10, butane  (b) C3H4, propyne**
