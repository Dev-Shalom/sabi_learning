## Question

Calculate the relative molecular mass of (a) calcium hydroxide, Ca(OH)2, (b) hydrated copper(II) tetraoxosulphate(VI), CuSO4.5H2O. [H = 1, O = 16, S = 32, Ca = 40, Cu = 64]

## Solution

1. The relative molecular mass is the sum of the relative atomic masses of all the atoms shown in the formula. A bracket multiplies everything inside it.
2. Part (a). Ca(OH)2 has one Ca and two OH groups, so it is 40 + 2 x (16 + 1).
3. = 40 + (2 x 17) = 40 + 34 = 74.
4. Part (b). The dot in CuSO4.5H2O means five water molecules are part of the crystal, so they count towards the mass.
5. CuSO4 = 64 + 32 + (4 x 16) = 160.
6. 5H2O = 5 x ((2 x 1) + 16) = 5 x 18 = 90.
7. Total = 160 + 90 = 250.
8. Relative molecular mass has no unit, being a ratio of masses. The same number in grams per mole is the molar mass.

**Answer: (a) 74  (b) 250**
