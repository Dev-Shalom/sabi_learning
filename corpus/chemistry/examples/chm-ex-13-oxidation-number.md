## Question

Determine the oxidation number of (a) chromium in potassium heptaoxodichromate(VI), K2Cr2O7, (b) nitrogen in the ammonium ion, NH4+.

## Solution

1. Use the standard rules: potassium, a Group I metal, is +1; oxygen is -2; hydrogen in a compound is +1; the oxidation numbers in a neutral compound add to zero, and in a polyatomic ion they add to the charge on the ion.
2. Part (a). Let the oxidation number of chromium be x. K2Cr2O7 is neutral, and there are two chromium atoms and seven oxygen atoms.
3. 2(+1) + 2x + 7(-2) = 0.
4. 2 + 2x - 14 = 0, so 2x = 12.
5. x = +6.
6. Part (b). Let the oxidation number of nitrogen be y. NH4+ carries a charge of +1, so the oxidation numbers add to +1.
7. y + 4(+1) = +1.
8. y = +1 - 4 = -3.
9. An oxidation number is not the same thing as a charge on an atom. It is a bookkeeping device, and it can be negative, positive or fractional.

**Answer: (a) +6  (b) -3**
