## Question

380 cm3 of a gas is collected at 27 degrees Celsius and 800 mmHg. Calculate its volume at s.t.p., which is 273 K and 760 mmHg.

## Solution

1. For a fixed mass of gas the general gas equation applies: P1V1 / T1 = P2V2 / T2. The temperatures must be in kelvin.
2. T1 = 27 + 273 = 300 K. T2 = 273 K.
3. Both pressures are already in mmHg, so they may be used as they are.
4. Rearranging for V2: V2 = P1V1T2 / (T1P2).
5. V2 = (800 x 380 x 273) / (300 x 760).
6. Taking the pressure factor first: 800 / 760 = 1.0526, and the temperature factor: 273 / 300 = 0.91.
7. V2 = 380 x 1.0526 x 0.91 = 364 cm3.
8. The two changes pull opposite ways. The pressure falls from 800 to 760 mmHg, which on its own would expand the gas by a factor of 1.0526; the temperature falls from 300 K to 273 K, which on its own would shrink it by a factor of 0.91. The cooling is the larger effect, so the corrected volume is smaller than 380 cm3.

**Answer: 364 cm3**
