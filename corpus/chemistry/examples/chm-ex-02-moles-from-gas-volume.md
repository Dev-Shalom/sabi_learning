## Question

A sample of carbon(IV) oxide, CO2, occupies 5.6 dm3 at s.t.p. Calculate (a) the number of moles present, (b) the mass of the sample. [C = 12, O = 16; molar volume of a gas at s.t.p. = 22.4 dm3/mol]

## Solution

1. Part (a). At s.t.p. one mole of any gas occupies 22.4 dm3, so number of moles = volume in dm3 / 22.4.
2. Number of moles = 5.6 / 22.4 = 0.25 mol.
3. Part (b). Mass = number of moles x molar mass.
4. Molar mass of CO2 = 12 + (2 x 16) = 44 g/mol.
5. Mass = 0.25 x 44 = 11 g.
6. The molar volume applies only at s.t.p., which is 273 K and 760 mmHg. A volume measured at other conditions must be corrected first.

**Answer: (a) 0.25 mol  (b) 11 g**
