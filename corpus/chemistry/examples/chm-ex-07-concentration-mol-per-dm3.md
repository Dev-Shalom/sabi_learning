## Question

7.45 g of potassium chloride, KCl, is dissolved in water and made up to 250 cm3 of solution. Calculate the concentration of the solution in mol/dm3. [Cl = 35.5, K = 39]

## Solution

1. Concentration in mol/dm3 is the number of moles of solute in one cubic decimetre of solution, so find the moles and the volume in dm3.
2. Molar mass of KCl = 39 + 35.5 = 74.5 g/mol.
3. Moles of KCl = 7.45 / 74.5 = 0.1 mol.
4. Volume in dm3 = 250 / 1000 = 0.25 dm3, since 1 dm3 = 1000 cm3.
5. Concentration = 0.1 / 0.25 = 0.4 mol/dm3.
6. Note that the volume is the volume of the solution, not the volume of water added. The solid is dissolved in a little water and then made up to the mark.

**Answer: 0.4 mol/dm3**
