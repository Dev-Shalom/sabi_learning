## Question

Sulphur(IV) oxide and oxygen reach equilibrium in a closed vessel of volume 1 dm3 according to

2SO2(g) + O2(g) reversible 2SO3(g)

At equilibrium the vessel contains 0.2 mol/dm3 of SO2, 0.1 mol/dm3 of O2 and 0.4 mol/dm3 of SO3. Write the expression for Kc and calculate its value, with its unit.

## Solution

1. Products go on top, reactants underneath, each concentration raised to the power of its coefficient in the balanced equation.
2. Kc = [SO3]^2 / ([SO2]^2 x [O2])
3. Substitute the equilibrium concentrations: Kc = (0.4)^2 / ((0.2)^2 x 0.1).
4. The top is 0.16 and the bottom is 0.04 x 0.1 = 0.004.
5. Kc = 0.16 / 0.004 = 40.
6. For the unit, put mol/dm3 into the expression: (mol/dm3)^2 divided by (mol/dm3)^2 x (mol/dm3) leaves 1 / (mol/dm3), which is dm3/mol.
7. Kc = 40 dm3/mol. It is large, so at this temperature the equilibrium lies well over towards the sulphur(VI) oxide.
8. Kc is changed only by temperature. Adding a catalyst, such as the vanadium(V) oxide used in the Contact process, brings equilibrium on sooner but leaves Kc alone.

**Answer: Kc = [SO3]^2 / ([SO2]^2 x [O2]), and its value is 40 dm3/mol**
