## Question

Ethyl ethanoate, CH3COOC2H5, is the alkanoate formed when ethanol reacts with ethanoic acid. Calculate (a) its relative molecular mass, (b) the percentage by mass of carbon in it, to two decimal places. [H = 1, C = 12, O = 16]

## Solution

1. Part (a). First count the atoms in the condensed formula. CH3COOC2H5 has 4 carbon atoms, 8 hydrogen atoms and 2 oxygen atoms, so its molecular formula is C4H8O2.
2. Relative molecular mass = (4 x 12) + (8 x 1) + (2 x 16).
3. = 48 + 8 + 32 = 88.
4. Part (b). The percentage by mass of an element is the mass that element contributes, divided by the relative molecular mass, times 100.
5. Mass contributed by carbon = 4 x 12 = 48.
6. Percentage of carbon = (48 / 88) x 100 = 54.55 per cent.
7. Check the three percentages add to 100. Carbon 54.55, hydrogen 9.09, oxygen 36.36, and 54.55 + 9.09 + 36.36 = 100 to two decimal places.

**Answer: (a) 88  (b) 54.55 per cent carbon**
