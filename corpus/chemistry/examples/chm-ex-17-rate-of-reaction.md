## Question

In a reaction the concentration of one of the reactants falls from 0.5 mol/dm3 to 0.2 mol/dm3 in 60 seconds. Calculate the average rate of the reaction over that interval, and state its unit.

## Solution

1. The average rate is the change in the amount of a reactant or product divided by the time taken.
2. Change in concentration = 0.5 - 0.2 = 0.3 mol/dm3.
3. Average rate = 0.3 / 60.
4. Average rate = 0.005 mol/dm3 per second.
5. The rate is quoted as a positive number even though the concentration fell, because rate means how fast the change happens.
6. This is an average over the whole interval. The actual rate was highest at the start, when the reactant was most concentrated, and fell as it was used up.

**Answer: 0.005 mol/dm3 per second**
