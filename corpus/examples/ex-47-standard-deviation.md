## Question

Find the variance and the standard deviation of the numbers 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.

## Solution

1. Mean = (2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10) divided by 5 = 30/5 = 6.
2. Find each deviation from the mean: -4, -2, 0, 2, 4.
3. Square each deviation: 16, 4, 0, 4, 16.
4. Sum of squared deviations = 40.
5. Variance = 40 divided by 5 = 8.
6. Standard deviation = the square root of 8 = 2.828.

**Answer: variance 8; standard deviation 2.83 (to 2 decimal places).**

## Common mistake

Stopping at the variance. If the question asks for standard deviation, the square
root at the end is compulsory, and it is the step most often forgotten.
