## Question

y varies directly as the square of x and inversely as z. When x = 4 and z = 2,
y = 8. Find y when x = 6 and z = 3.

## Solution

1. Write the relation with a constant: y = k x^2 divided by z.
2. Substitute the known values: 8 = k(4^2) divided by 2 = 16k/2 = 8k.
3. So k = 1.
4. The relation is y = x^2 divided by z.
5. When x = 6 and z = 3: y = 36 divided by 3 = 12.

**Answer: y = 12.**

## Common mistake

Substituting the new values before finding k. The constant must be found first,
from the pair of values given.
