## Question

Simplify 27^(2/3) times 16^(-1/2).

## Solution

1. Deal with 27^(2/3). The denominator 3 means take the cube root, the numerator
   2 means square the result. The cube root of 27 is 3, and 3 squared is 9.
2. Deal with 16^(-1/2). The negative sign means take the reciprocal, and the half
   means take the square root. The square root of 16 is 4, so 16^(-1/2) = 1/4.
3. Multiply: 9 times 1/4 = 9/4.

**Answer: 9/4, or 2.25.**

## Common mistake

Treating a negative index as making the answer negative. A negative index gives a
reciprocal, never a negative number.
