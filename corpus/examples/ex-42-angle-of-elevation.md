## Question

From a point on level ground 50 m from the foot of a building, the angle of
elevation of the top of the building is 32 degrees. Find the height of the
building, correct to one decimal place.

## Solution

1. Draw the right-angled triangle: the 50 m is the side adjacent to the angle,
   and the height is the side opposite.
2. Opposite over adjacent is the tangent, so tan 32 = height divided by 50.
3. Height = 50 times tan 32.
4. tan 32 = 0.6249.
5. Height = 50 times 0.6249 = 31.245.

**Answer: 31.2 m (to 1 decimal place).**

## Common mistake

Using sine instead of tangent. Sine needs the hypotenuse, which is the line of
sight, and that length was not given.
