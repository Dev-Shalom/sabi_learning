## Question

The cash price of a generator is 120,000 naira. On hire purchase a buyer pays a
deposit of 30,000 naira and 12 monthly instalments of 9,000 naira each. Find the
hire purchase price, and express the extra charge as a percentage of the cash
price.

## Solution

1. Total instalments = 12 times 9,000 = 108,000 naira.
2. Hire purchase price = deposit + instalments = 30,000 + 108,000 = 138,000 naira.
3. Extra charge = 138,000 - 120,000 = 18,000 naira.
4. As a percentage of the cash price: (18,000 divided by 120,000) times 100.
5. = 15.

**Answer: hire purchase price 138,000 naira; extra charge 15 per cent of the cash
price.**

## Common mistake

Working the percentage on the hire purchase price instead of the cash price. The
question asks for a percentage of the cash price, so that is the denominator.
