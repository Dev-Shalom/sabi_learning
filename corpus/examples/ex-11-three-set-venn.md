## Question

In a survey of 100 students, 60 like rice, 50 like beans and 40 like yam. 30 like
both rice and beans, 25 like both rice and yam, 20 like both beans and yam, and
10 like all three. How many students like none of the three foods?

## Solution

1. Use the three-set formula:
   n(R union B union Y) = n(R) + n(B) + n(Y) - n(R and B) - n(R and Y)
   - n(B and Y) + n(R and B and Y).
2. Substitute: = 60 + 50 + 40 - 30 - 25 - 20 + 10.
3. = 150 - 75 + 10 = 85.
4. Students who like none = total minus the union = 100 - 85 = 15.

**Answer: 15 students like none of the three.**

## Common mistake

Forgetting to add back the centre. Students in all three sets are subtracted three
times by the pairwise terms, so they must be added back once.
