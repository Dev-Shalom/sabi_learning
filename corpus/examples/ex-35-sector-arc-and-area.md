## Question

A sector of a circle of radius 14 cm subtends an angle of 90 degrees at the
centre. Taking pi as 22/7, find (a) the length of the arc, (b) the area of the
sector.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. Length of arc = (theta over 360) times 2 pi r.
2. = (90/360) times 2 times (22/7) times 14.
3. 2 times (22/7) times 14 = 88, and 90/360 = 1/4.
4. Arc = 88 divided by 4 = 22 cm.

**Answer (a): 22 cm.**

Part (b)

5. Area of sector = (theta over 360) times pi r squared.
6. = (1/4) times (22/7) times 196.
7. (22/7) times 196 = 616.
8. Area = 616 divided by 4 = 154 cm squared.

**Answer (b): 154 cm squared.**
