## Question

Find the area bounded by the curve y = 4 - x^2 and the x axis.

## Solution

1. Find where the curve meets the x axis: 4 - x^2 = 0, so x = -2 or x = 2. These
   are the limits.
2. Between these limits the curve lies above the axis, so a single integral gives
   the area.
3. Integrate: the integral of (4 - x^2) is 4x - x^3/3.
4. At x = 2: 8 - 8/3 = 16/3.
5. At x = -2: -8 + 8/3 = -16/3.
6. Area = 16/3 - (-16/3) = 32/3.

**Answer: 32/3 square units, which is 10.67 to two decimal places.**

## Note

The limits were not given in the question. When a question says "bounded by the
curve and the x axis", the roots of the equation are the limits.
