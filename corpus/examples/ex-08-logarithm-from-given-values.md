## Question

Given that log 2 = 0.3010 and log 3 = 0.4771, evaluate log 12 without using
tables.

## Solution

1. Write 12 as a product of 2s and 3s: 12 = 4 times 3 = 2^2 times 3.
2. Apply the product law: log 12 = log(2^2) + log 3.
3. Apply the power law: log(2^2) = 2 log 2 = 2 times 0.3010 = 0.6020.
4. Add: log 12 = 0.6020 + 0.4771 = 1.0791.

**Answer: 1.0791.**

## Why the answer is a little more than 1

log 10 = 1, and 12 is a little more than 10, so a value just above 1 is the sense
check.
