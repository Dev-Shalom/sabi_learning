## Question

The 5th term of an arithmetic progression is 11 and the 9th term is 23. Find
(a) the first term and the common difference, (b) the sum of the first 20 terms.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. The nth term is T(n) = a + (n - 1)d.
2. 5th term: a + 4d = 11.
3. 9th term: a + 8d = 23.
4. Subtract the first from the second: 4d = 12, so d = 3.
5. Substitute back: a + 12 = 11, so a = -1.

**Answer (a): first term -1, common difference 3.**

Part (b)

6. S(n) = (n/2)[2a + (n - 1)d].
7. S(20) = (20/2)[2(-1) + 19(3)] = 10[-2 + 57] = 10(55).
8. = 550.

**Answer (b): 550.**
