## Question

The table shows the marks scored by 40 students.

Marks 1-5, frequency 4.
Marks 6-10, frequency 8.
Marks 11-15, frequency 12.
Marks 16-20, frequency 10.
Marks 21-25, frequency 6.

Calculate the mean mark, and state the modal class.

## Solution

1. Find the mid-value of each class, which is half the sum of its lower and upper
   limits: 3, 8, 13, 18 and 23.
2. Multiply each mid-value by its frequency:
   4 times 3 = 12;
   8 times 8 = 64;
   12 times 13 = 156;
   10 times 18 = 180;
   6 times 23 = 138.
3. Sum of fx = 12 + 64 + 156 + 180 + 138 = 550.
4. Sum of f = 40.
5. Mean = 550 divided by 40 = 13.75.
6. The modal class is the class with the highest frequency, which is 11-15 with a
   frequency of 12.

**Answer: mean 13.75; modal class 11-15.**

## Common mistake

Dividing by the number of classes instead of by the total frequency. The
denominator is always the sum of the frequencies.
