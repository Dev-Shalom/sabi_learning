## Question

A man invests 50,000 naira at 10 per cent per annum compound interest. Find the
amount after 3 years, and the compound interest earned.

## Solution

1. Amount A = P(1 + R/100)^n.
2. A = 50000 times (1 + 10/100)^3 = 50000 times (1.1)^3.
3. (1.1)^3 = 1.331.
4. A = 50000 times 1.331 = 66,550.
5. Compound interest = A - P = 66550 - 50000 = 16,550.

**Answer: amount 66,550 naira; compound interest 16,550 naira.**

## Compare with simple interest

Simple interest over the same period would be 15,000 naira. The extra 1,550 naira
is interest earned on interest, which is the whole point of the compound case.
