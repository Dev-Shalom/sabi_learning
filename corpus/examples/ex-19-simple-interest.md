## Question

Find the sum of money that will earn a simple interest of 4,500 naira in 3 years
at 6 per cent per annum.

## Solution

1. Simple interest I = PRT divided by 100.
2. Make P the subject: P = 100I divided by RT.
3. Substitute I = 4500, R = 6, T = 3.
4. P = (100 times 4500) divided by (6 times 3) = 450000 divided by 18.
5. = 25000.

**Answer: 25,000 naira.**

## Check

Interest on 25,000 naira for one year at 6 per cent is 1,500 naira, and over
3 years that is 4,500 naira.
