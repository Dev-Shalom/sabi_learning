## Question

A cone has base radius 7 cm and vertical height 24 cm. Taking pi as 22/7, find
(a) the slant height, (b) the volume, (c) the total surface area.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. The slant height l satisfies l squared = r squared + h squared.
2. l squared = 49 + 576 = 625, so l = 25 cm.

**Answer (a): 25 cm.**

Part (b)

3. Volume = one third pi r squared h.
4. = (1/3) times (22/7) times 49 times 24.
5. (22/7) times 49 = 154; 154 times 24 = 3696; 3696 divided by 3 = 1232.

**Answer (b): 1232 cm cubed.**

Part (c)

6. Curved surface area = pi r l = (22/7) times 7 times 25 = 550 cm squared.
7. Base area = pi r squared = (22/7) times 49 = 154 cm squared.
8. Total surface area = 550 + 154 = 704 cm squared.

**Answer (c): 704 cm squared.**

## Common mistake

Using the vertical height in the curved surface formula. The curved surface uses
the slant height; the volume uses the vertical height.
