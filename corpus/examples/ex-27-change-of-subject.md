## Question

Make g the subject of the formula T = 2 pi times the square root of (l divided by
g).

## Solution

1. Divide both sides by 2 pi: T divided by 2 pi = the square root of (l/g).
2. Square both sides: T^2 divided by 4 pi^2 = l divided by g.
3. Take the reciprocal of both sides: 4 pi^2 divided by T^2 = g divided by l.
4. Multiply both sides by l: g = 4 pi^2 l divided by T^2.

**Answer: g = 4 pi squared times l, divided by T squared.**

## Common mistake

Squaring only one side, or forgetting that squaring must be applied to the whole
of each side, not term by term.
