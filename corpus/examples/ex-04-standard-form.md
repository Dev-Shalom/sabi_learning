## Question

Express 0.0004536 (a) in standard form, (b) correct to two significant figures.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. Move the decimal point until there is exactly one non-zero digit in front of
   it. Moving it four places to the right gives 4.536.
2. Because the point moved four places to the right, the power of ten is
   negative four.

**Answer (a): 4.536 times 10 to the power negative 4.**

Part (b)

3. The first significant figure is 4, the second is 5. Look at the next digit,
   which is 3.
4. Since 3 is less than 5, the second significant figure stays as it is.

**Answer (b): 0.00045.**

## Common mistake

Counting the leading zeros as significant figures. Zeros before the first
non-zero digit never count.
