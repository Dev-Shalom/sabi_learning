## Question

The 3rd term of a geometric progression is 12 and the 6th term is 96. Find
(a) the common ratio and the first term, (b) the sum of the first 8 terms.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. The nth term is T(n) = a r^(n - 1).
2. 3rd term: a r^2 = 12.
3. 6th term: a r^5 = 96.
4. Divide the second by the first: r^3 = 96/12 = 8, so r = 2.
5. Substitute into a r^2 = 12: 4a = 12, so a = 3.

**Answer (a): common ratio 2, first term 3.**

Part (b)

6. S(n) = a(r^n - 1) divided by (r - 1), used because r is greater than 1.
7. S(8) = 3(2^8 - 1) divided by (2 - 1) = 3(256 - 1) divided by 1.
8. = 3 times 255 = 765.

**Answer (b): 765.**
