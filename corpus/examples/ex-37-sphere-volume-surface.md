## Question

A sphere has radius 10.5 cm. Taking pi as 22/7, find (a) its volume, (b) its
surface area.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. Volume = four thirds pi r cubed.
2. Write 10.5 as 21/2, so r cubed = 9261/8.
3. Volume = (4/3) times (22/7) times (9261/8).
4. (4/3) times (22/7) = 88/21.
5. (88/21) times (9261/8) = 4851.

**Answer (a): 4851 cm cubed.**

Part (b)

6. Surface area = 4 pi r squared, and r squared = 110.25.
7. = 4 times (22/7) times 110.25 = (88/7) times 110.25.
8. = 1386.

**Answer (b): 1386 cm squared.**

## Note

Using 21/2 instead of 10.5 keeps the arithmetic exact and lets the 7 in 22/7
cancel, which is why WAEC chooses radii like this.
