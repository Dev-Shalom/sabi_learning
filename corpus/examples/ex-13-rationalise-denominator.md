## Question

Express 5 divided by (3 - root 2) in the form a + b root 2, where a and b are
rational numbers.

## Solution

1. The conjugate of 3 - root 2 is 3 + root 2. Multiply top and bottom by it.
2. Numerator: 5(3 + root 2) = 15 + 5 root 2.
3. Denominator: (3 - root 2)(3 + root 2) = 9 - 2 = 7, using the difference of two
   squares.
4. So the expression is (15 + 5 root 2) divided by 7.
5. Split it: 15/7 + (5/7) root 2.

**Answer: 15/7 + (5/7) root 2.**

## Common mistake

Multiplying by the same expression instead of the conjugate. Multiplying by
(3 - root 2) leaves a surd in the denominator.
