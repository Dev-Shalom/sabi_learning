## Question

Find (a) the sum of the interior angles of a polygon with 12 sides, (b) the size
of each interior angle if the polygon is regular.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. The sum of the interior angles of a polygon with n sides is (n - 2) times 180
   degrees.
2. With n = 12: sum = (12 - 2) times 180 = 10 times 180.
3. = 1800 degrees.

**Answer (a): 1800 degrees.**

Part (b)

4. In a regular polygon all interior angles are equal, so divide by the number of
   sides: 1800 divided by 12 = 150 degrees.

**Answer (b): 150 degrees.**

## Alternative for part (b)

Each exterior angle of a regular 12-sided polygon is 360 divided by 12 = 30
degrees, and the interior angle is 180 - 30 = 150 degrees.
