## Question

Find the sum to infinity of the geometric progression 18, 6, 2, ...

## Solution

1. Find the common ratio: r = 6 divided by 18 = 1/3. Check with the next pair:
   2 divided by 6 = 1/3, so the sequence is geometric.
2. The sum to infinity exists only if r lies strictly between -1 and 1. Here
   r = 1/3, so it exists.
3. S(infinity) = a divided by (1 - r) = 18 divided by (1 - 1/3).
4. = 18 divided by (2/3) = 18 times 3/2.
5. = 27.

**Answer: 27.**

## Note

If the common ratio had been 3 instead of 1/3, there would be no sum to infinity,
and the correct answer would be to say the series does not converge.
