## Question

If alpha and beta are the roots of 2x^2 - 5x + 1 = 0, find (a) alpha + beta and
alpha times beta, (b) the value of 1/alpha + 1/beta.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. For ax^2 + bx + c = 0, the sum of the roots is -b/a and the product is c/a.
2. Here a = 2, b = -5, c = 1.
3. Sum: alpha + beta = 5/2.
4. Product: alpha times beta = 1/2.

**Answer (a): sum 5/2, product 1/2.**

Part (b)

5. 1/alpha + 1/beta = (beta + alpha) divided by (alpha times beta).
6. = (5/2) divided by (1/2).
7. = 5.

**Answer (b): 5.**

## Note

The roots themselves are irrational here, so working through the sum and product
is much faster than solving the equation first.
