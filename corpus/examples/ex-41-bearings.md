## Question

A ship sails 60 km from port P on a bearing of 045 degrees to point Q, then 80 km
from Q on a bearing of 135 degrees to point R. Find (a) the distance PR, (b) the
bearing of R from P, correct to the nearest degree.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. The two bearings differ by 135 - 45 = 90 degrees, so the angle PQR is 90
   degrees. (The north line at Q makes the interior angle a right angle.)
2. With a right angle at Q, use Pythagoras:
   PR squared = 60 squared + 80 squared = 3600 + 6400 = 10000.
3. PR = 100 km.

**Answer (a): 100 km.**

Part (b)

4. In right-angled triangle PQR, tan(angle QPR) = 80 divided by 60 = 1.3333.
5. Angle QPR = 53.13 degrees.
6. The bearing of R from P is the bearing of Q from P plus this angle:
   45 + 53.13 = 98.13 degrees.

**Answer (b): 098 degrees.**

## Note

A bearing is always written with three figures, so the answer is 098 degrees, not
98 degrees.
