## Question

A bag contains 5 red, 3 blue and 2 green balls. One ball is drawn at random.
(a) Find the probability that it is red or green.
Two balls are now drawn one after the other without replacement.
(b) Find the probability that both are red.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. Total number of balls = 5 + 3 + 2 = 10.
2. Red or green are mutually exclusive, so add the probabilities:
   P(red) + P(green) = 5/10 + 2/10 = 7/10.

**Answer (a): 7/10.**

Part (b)

3. First draw: P(red) = 5/10.
4. Without replacement, one red has gone, so 4 reds remain out of 9 balls:
   P(red on the second draw) = 4/9.
5. Multiply: (5/10) times (4/9) = 20/90 = 2/9.

**Answer (b): 2/9.**

## If it had been with replacement

The second probability would stay at 5/10, giving (5/10) times (5/10) = 1/4. One
word in the question changes the whole answer.
