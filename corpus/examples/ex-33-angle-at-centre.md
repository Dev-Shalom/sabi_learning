## Question

In a circle with centre O, the points A, B and C lie on the circumference. The
angle AOB at the centre is 130 degrees. Find the angle ACB, giving a reason.

## Solution

1. Identify the theorem: the angle subtended by an arc at the centre of a circle
   is twice the angle subtended by the same arc at any point on the remaining part
   of the circumference.
2. Both angles stand on the same arc AB. So angle AOB = 2 times angle ACB.
3. 130 = 2 times angle ACB.
4. Angle ACB = 65 degrees.

**Answer: angle ACB = 65 degrees (angle at the centre is twice the angle at the
circumference).**

## In the exam

Write the reason next to the answer. In Paper 2, the reason carries marks of its
own.
