## Question

Evaluate the integral of (3x^2 - 4x + 5) with respect to x, from x = 1 to x = 3.

## Solution

1. Integrate term by term. The integral of ax^n is a x^(n+1) divided by (n + 1).
2. The integral of 3x^2 is x^3; of -4x is -2x^2; of 5 is 5x.
3. So the indefinite integral is x^3 - 2x^2 + 5x. The constant of integration is
   not written for a definite integral because it cancels.
4. Substitute the upper limit x = 3: 27 - 18 + 15 = 24.
5. Substitute the lower limit x = 1: 1 - 2 + 5 = 4.
6. Subtract: 24 - 4 = 20.

**Answer: 20.**

## Common mistake

Subtracting in the wrong order. It is always the value at the upper limit minus
the value at the lower limit.
