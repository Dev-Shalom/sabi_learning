## Question

In triangle ABC, angle A = 45 degrees, angle B = 60 degrees and side a = 10 cm.
Find side b, correct to one decimal place.

## Solution

1. Two angles and the side opposite one of them are given, so use the sine rule.
2. a over sin A = b over sin B.
3. 10 over sin 45 = b over sin 60.
4. b = 10 times sin 60 divided by sin 45.
5. sin 60 = 0.8660 and sin 45 = 0.7071.
6. b = 8.660 divided by 0.7071 = 12.247.

**Answer: b = 12.2 cm (to 1 decimal place).**

## Sense check

Angle B is larger than angle A, so side b must be longer than side a. It is.
