## Question

PQRS is a cyclic quadrilateral in which angle P = 78 degrees and angle Q = 95
degrees. Find angles R and S.

## Solution

1. The opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add up to 180 degrees.
2. P and R are opposite: 78 + angle R = 180, so angle R = 102 degrees.
3. Q and S are opposite: 95 + angle S = 180, so angle S = 85 degrees.

**Answer: angle R = 102 degrees, angle S = 85 degrees.**

## Check

All four angles of any quadrilateral add to 360 degrees:
78 + 95 + 102 + 85 = 360.
