## Question

Each exterior angle of a regular polygon is 24 degrees. Find the number of sides
and the sum of the interior angles.

## Solution

1. The exterior angles of any polygon add up to 360 degrees.
2. For a regular polygon, number of sides n = 360 divided by the exterior angle.
3. n = 360 divided by 24 = 15.
4. Sum of interior angles = (n - 2) times 180 = 13 times 180.
5. = 2340 degrees.

**Answer: 15 sides; sum of interior angles 2340 degrees.**

## Check

Each interior angle is 180 - 24 = 156 degrees, and 15 times 156 = 2340 degrees.
