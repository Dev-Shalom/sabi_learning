## Question

The point P has coordinates (3, -5). Find the image of P under
(a) a reflection in the line y = x,
(b) a rotation of 90 degrees anticlockwise about the origin,
(c) a translation by the vector with components -2 and 4.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. Reflection in the line y = x swaps the coordinates: (x, y) maps to (y, x).
2. So (3, -5) maps to (-5, 3).

**Answer (a): (-5, 3).**

Part (b)

3. A rotation of 90 degrees anticlockwise about the origin maps (x, y) to
   (-y, x).
4. So (3, -5) maps to (5, 3).

**Answer (b): (5, 3).**

Part (c)

5. A translation adds the components: (x + a, y + b).
6. (3 + (-2), -5 + 4) = (1, -1).

**Answer (c): (1, -1).**

## Common mistake

Confusing reflection in y = x with rotation through 90 degrees. They give
different images, as parts (a) and (b) show.
