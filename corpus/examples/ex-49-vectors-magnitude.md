## Question

The points A and B have coordinates (2, 3) and (7, -9). Find (a) the vector AB in
component form, (b) the magnitude of AB.

## Solution

Part (a)

1. The vector AB is the position vector of B minus the position vector of A.
2. AB = (7 - 2, -9 - 3).
3. = (5, -12).

**Answer (a): AB has components 5 and -12.**

Part (b)

4. The magnitude is the square root of (x squared + y squared).
5. = the square root of (25 + 144) = the square root of 169.
6. = 13.

**Answer (b): 13 units.**

## Common mistake

Subtracting in the wrong order. AB is B minus A; BA would be A minus B and points
the other way, though it has the same magnitude.
