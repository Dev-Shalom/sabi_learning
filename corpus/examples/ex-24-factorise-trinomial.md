## Question

Factorise completely: 6x^2 - 7x - 3.

## Solution

1. Multiply the first coefficient by the constant: 6 times -3 = -18.
2. Find two numbers whose product is -18 and whose sum is -7. They are -9 and 2.
3. Split the middle term: 6x^2 - 9x + 2x - 3.
4. Factorise in pairs: 3x(2x - 3) + 1(2x - 3).
5. Take out the common bracket: (3x + 1)(2x - 3).

**Answer: (3x + 1)(2x - 3).**

## Check

Expanding gives 6x^2 - 9x + 2x - 3 = 6x^2 - 7x - 3, which is the original.
