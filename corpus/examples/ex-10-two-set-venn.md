## Question

In a class of 40 students, 25 offer Mathematics, 20 offer Physics and 8 offer
neither subject. How many students offer both subjects?

## Solution

1. Students offering at least one subject = 40 - 8 = 32. So n(M union P) = 32.
2. Use n(M union P) = n(M) + n(P) - n(M intersection P).
3. 32 = 25 + 20 - n(M intersection P).
4. 32 = 45 - n(M intersection P).
5. n(M intersection P) = 45 - 32 = 13.

**Answer: 13 students offer both subjects.**

## Check

Maths only = 25 - 13 = 12, Physics only = 20 - 13 = 7. Then
12 + 7 + 13 + 8 = 40, which is the class size.
