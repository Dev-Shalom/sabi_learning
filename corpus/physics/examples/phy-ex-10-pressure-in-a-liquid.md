## Question

Find the pressure due to the water at a depth of 2.5 m below the surface of a tank. Take the density of water as 1000 kg/m³ and g = 10 m/s².

## Solution

1. The pressure due to a column of liquid is P = ρgh, where ρ is the density and h the depth.
2. P = 1000 × 10 × 2.5.
3. P = 25000 Pa.
4. This is the pressure due to the water alone. The total pressure at that depth also includes atmospheric pressure pushing down on the surface.

**Answer: 25000 Pa (25 kPa)**
