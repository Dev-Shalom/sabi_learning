## Question

An effort of 200 N raises a load of 600 N. The effort moves 4 m while the load rises 1 m. Find (a) the mechanical advantage, (b) the velocity ratio, (c) the efficiency of the machine.

## Solution

1. Part (a). Mechanical advantage is load divided by effort, M.A. = L / E.
2. M.A. = 600 / 200 = 3.
3. Part (b). Velocity ratio is the distance moved by the effort divided by the distance moved by the load.
4. V.R. = 4 / 1 = 4.
5. Part (c). Efficiency = (M.A. / V.R.) × 100%.
6. Efficiency = (3 / 4) × 100 = 75%.
7. It is below 100% because some of the effort is spent against friction and in lifting the moving parts of the machine itself.

**Answer: (a) 3  (b) 4  (c) 75%**
