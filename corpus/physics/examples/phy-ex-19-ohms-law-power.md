## Question

A 12 V battery of negligible internal resistance is connected across a 8 Ω resistor. Find (a) the current, (b) the power dissipated in the resistor.

## Solution

1. Part (a). Ohm's law gives V = IR, so I = V / R.
2. I = 12 / 8 = 1.5 A.
3. Part (b). Power is P = VI.
4. P = 12 × 1.5 = 18 W.
5. Checking with P = I²R: 1.5² × 8 = 18 W, which agrees.

**Answer: (a) 1.5 A  (b) 18 W**
