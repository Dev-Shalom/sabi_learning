## Question

An object is placed 30 cm from a converging lens of focal length 10 cm. Find (a) the image distance, (b) the magnification.

## Solution

1. Part (a). The lens formula is 1/f = 1/u + 1/v, so 1/v = 1/f − 1/u.
2. 1/v = 1/10 − 1/30 = 0.1 − 0.0333 = 0.0667.
3. v = 15 cm. It is positive, so the image is real and on the far side of the lens from the object.
4. Part (b). Magnification m = v / u.
5. m = 15 / 30 = 0.5.
6. The magnification is less than 1, so the image is smaller than the object, and being real it is inverted.

**Answer: (a) 15 cm  (b) 0.5**
