## Question

A trolley of mass 3 kg moving at 4 m/s collides with a stationary trolley of mass 2 kg. They stick together after the collision. Find their common velocity.

## Solution

1. Momentum is conserved in a collision, so total momentum before equals total momentum after.
2. Before: p = m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = (3 × 4) + (2 × 0) = 12 kg m/s.
3. After: the two move together with a combined mass of 3 + 2 = 5 kg, so p = 5v.
4. Setting them equal: 5v = 12.
5. v = 12 / 5 = 2.4 m/s.

**Answer: 2.4 m/s in the direction of the first trolley**
