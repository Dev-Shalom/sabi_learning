## Question

A 6 Ω resistor and a 3 Ω resistor are connected in parallel, and that combination is connected in series with a 4 Ω resistor. Find the total resistance of the circuit.

## Solution

1. Take the parallel pair first. For resistors in parallel, 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂.
2. 1/R = 1/6 + 1/3 = 0.1667 + 0.3333 = 0.5.
3. R = 2 Ω for the parallel section.
4. Resistors in series simply add.
5. Total = 2 + 4 = 6 Ω.

**Answer: 6 Ω**
