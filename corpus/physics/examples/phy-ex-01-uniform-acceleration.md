## Question

A car starts from rest and reaches a speed of 20 m/s in 8 s. Find (a) its acceleration, (b) the distance it covers in that time.

## Solution

1. Write down what is given: u = 0 m/s, v = 20 m/s, t = 8 s.
2. Part (a). Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity, a = (v − u) / t.
3. a = (20 − 0) / 8 = 2.5 m/s².
4. Part (b). With uniform acceleration from rest, s = ut + ½at².
5. s = 0 + ½ × 2.5 × 8² = ½ × 2.5 × 64.
6. s = 80 m.

**Answer: (a) 2.5 m/s²  (b) 80 m**
