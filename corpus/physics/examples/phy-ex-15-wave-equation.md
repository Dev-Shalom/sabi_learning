## Question

A wave has a frequency of 50 Hz and a wavelength of 6 m. Find (a) its speed, (b) its period.

## Solution

1. Part (a). The wave equation is v = fλ.
2. v = 50 × 6 = 300 m/s.
3. Part (b). The period is the time for one complete wave, T = 1 / f.
4. T = 1 / 50 = 0.02 s.

**Answer: (a) 300 m/s  (b) 0.02 s**
