## Question

A stone is dropped from rest from a height of 45 m. Taking g = 10 m/s² and ignoring air resistance, find (a) the time it takes to reach the ground, (b) the speed with which it lands.

## Solution

1. Given: u = 0 m/s, h = 45 m, g = 10 m/s².
2. Part (a). Falling from rest, h = ½gt², so t = √(2h / g).
3. t = √(2 × 45 / 10) = √9.
4. t = 3 s.
5. Part (b). The speed on landing is v = gt.
6. v = 10 × 3 = 30 m/s.

**Answer: (a) 3 s  (b) 30 m/s**
