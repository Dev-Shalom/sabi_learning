## Question

A ball of mass 0.5 kg is held 20 m above the ground and released. Taking g = 10 m/s² and ignoring air resistance, find (a) its potential energy before release, (b) its speed as it reaches the ground.

## Solution

1. Part (a). Potential energy is P.E. = mgh.
2. P.E. = 0.5 × 10 × 20 = 100 J.
3. Part (b). All of that becomes kinetic energy at the ground, so ½mv² = mgh. The mass cancels, leaving v = √(2gh).
4. v = √(2 × 10 × 20) = √400.
5. v = 20 m/s.

**Answer: (a) 100 J  (b) 20 m/s**
