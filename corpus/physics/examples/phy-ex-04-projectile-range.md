## Question

A ball is projected from level ground at 40 m/s at 30° to the horizontal. Taking g = 10 m/s², find (a) its time of flight, (b) its horizontal range.

## Solution

1. Resolve the launch velocity. Vertical: u sin30 = 40 × 0.5 = 20 m/s. Horizontal: u cos30 = 40 × 0.866 = 34.64 m/s.
2. Part (a). Over level ground the time of flight is T = 2u sinθ / g.
3. T = (2 × 40 × 0.5) / 10 = 4 s.
4. Part (b). The horizontal velocity does not change, so R = u cosθ × T.
5. R = 34.64 × 4 = 138.56 m.

**Answer: (a) 4 s  (b) 138.56 m (about 139 m)**
