## Question

How much heat is needed to raise the temperature of 2 kg of water by 30 °C? Take the specific heat capacity of water as 4200 J/kg/K.

## Solution

1. The heat needed is Q = mcΔθ, where m is the mass, c the specific heat capacity and Δθ the temperature rise.
2. Q = 2 × 4200 × 30.
3. Q = 252000 J.
4. That is 252 kJ.

**Answer: 252000 J, or 252 kJ**
