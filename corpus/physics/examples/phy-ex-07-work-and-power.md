## Question

A man pushes a crate 12 m along the floor with a steady force of 250 N in the direction of motion, taking 6 s. Find (a) the work he does, (b) the power he develops.

## Solution

1. Part (a). Work is force times distance moved in the direction of the force, W = Fd.
2. W = 250 × 12 = 3000 J.
3. Part (b). Power is the rate of doing work, P = W / t.
4. P = 3000 / 6 = 500 W.

**Answer: (a) 3000 J  (b) 500 W**
