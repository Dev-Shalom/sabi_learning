## Question

A bus accelerates uniformly from rest to 15 m/s in 4 s, travels at that speed for 6 s, then decelerates uniformly to rest in a further 5 s. Find the total distance it covers.

## Solution

1. On a velocity-time graph the distance is the area under the line, so take the journey in three parts.
2. Accelerating: a triangle of base 4 s and height 15 m/s, area = ½ × 4 × 15 = 30 m.
3. Constant speed: a rectangle, area = 15 × 6 = 90 m.
4. Decelerating: a triangle of base 5 s and height 15 m/s, area = ½ × 5 × 15 = 37.5 m.
5. Total distance = 30 + 90 + 37.5.
6. = 157.5 m.

**Answer: 157.5 m**
