## Question

A transformer has 1000 turns on its primary coil and 250 turns on its secondary. The primary is connected to a 240 V a.c. supply. Find the secondary voltage, and say what kind of transformer it is.

## Solution

1. For an ideal transformer the turns ratio equals the voltage ratio: Vs / Vp = Ns / Np.
2. Vs = Vp × Ns / Np = 240 × 250 / 1000.
3. Vs = 60 V.
4. The secondary voltage is lower than the primary, so this is a step-down transformer.
5. A transformer only works on alternating current: a steady current produces no changing flux, so no e.m.f. is induced in the secondary.

**Answer: 60 V, a step-down transformer**
