## Question

The refractive index of a glass block is 1.5. Find the sine of the critical angle for light passing from the glass into air, and say what happens beyond that angle.

## Solution

1. At the critical angle the light refracts along the boundary, so the angle of refraction in air is 90°.
2. Snell's law gives n = 1 / sin C for light leaving a denser medium into air, so sin C = 1 / n.
3. sin C = 1 / 1.5 = 0.6667.
4. The critical angle is therefore about 42°.
5. At angles of incidence greater than the critical angle no light escapes: it is all reflected back into the glass. That is total internal reflection, and it is what makes an optical fibre work.

**Answer: sin C = 0.6667, so C ≈ 42°; beyond it, total internal reflection**
