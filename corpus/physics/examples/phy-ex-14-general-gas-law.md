## Question

A fixed mass of gas occupies 0.02 m³ at a pressure of 100 kPa and a temperature of 300 K. Find its volume when the pressure rises to 150 kPa and the temperature to 360 K.

## Solution

1. For a fixed mass of gas the general gas law applies: P₁V₁ / T₁ = P₂V₂ / T₂. Temperatures must be in kelvin, and they already are.
2. Rearranging for V₂: V₂ = P₁V₁T₂ / (T₁P₂).
3. V₂ = (100 × 0.02 × 360) / (300 × 150).
4. V₂ = 0.016 m³.
5. The volume falls because the increase in pressure outweighs the increase in temperature.

**Answer: 0.016 m³**
