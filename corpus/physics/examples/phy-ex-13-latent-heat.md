## Question

Find the heat needed to melt 0.4 kg of ice at 0 °C into water at 0 °C. Take the specific latent heat of fusion of ice as 336000 J/kg.

## Solution

1. Melting happens at constant temperature, so there is no mcΔθ term. The heat needed is Q = mL.
2. Q = 0.4 × 336000.
3. Q = 134400 J.
4. The temperature does not change while the ice melts: the energy goes into breaking the bonds holding the solid together, not into raising the temperature.

**Answer: 134400 J, or 134.4 kJ**
