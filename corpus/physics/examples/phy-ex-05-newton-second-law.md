## Question

A car of mass 1200 kg accelerates uniformly at 2.5 m/s². Find the resultant force acting on it.

## Solution

1. Newton's second law: the resultant force equals mass times acceleration, F = ma.
2. F = 1200 × 2.5.
3. F = 3000 N.
4. The force is in the same direction as the acceleration.

**Answer: 3000 N**
