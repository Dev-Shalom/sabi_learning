## Question

A metal block weighs 5 N in air and 3.2 N when fully immersed in water. Find (a) the upthrust on the block, (b) the weight of water it displaces.

## Solution

1. Part (a). The apparent loss in weight is the upthrust.
2. Upthrust = weight in air − weight in water = 5 − 3.2.
3. Upthrust = 1.8 N.
4. Part (b). By Archimedes' principle the upthrust equals the weight of the fluid displaced.
5. So the block displaces 1.8 N of water.

**Answer: (a) 1.8 N  (b) 1.8 N**
