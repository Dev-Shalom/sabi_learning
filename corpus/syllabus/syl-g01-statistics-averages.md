## What the syllabus requires

Collection and tabulation of data. Frequency distribution tables, including
grouped data. Bar charts, pie charts, histograms and frequency polygons. Mean,
median and mode for both ungrouped and grouped data.

## Ungrouped data

The mean is the sum of the values divided by how many there are. The median is the
middle value when the data is arranged in order; with an even number of values it
is the mean of the two middle ones. The mode is the value that occurs most often.

## Grouped data

The mean is the sum of fx divided by the sum of f, where x is the class mid-value
and f is the frequency.

The modal class is the class with the highest frequency. The mode of a grouped
distribution can be estimated from a histogram by joining the corners of the tallest
bar to the tops of its neighbours and reading off where the lines cross.

The median of grouped data is estimated from the cumulative frequency curve, at the
value corresponding to half the total frequency.

## Pie charts

The angle of a sector is the frequency divided by the total frequency, times 360
degrees. Check that all the angles add to 360.
