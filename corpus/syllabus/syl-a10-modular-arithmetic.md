## What the syllabus requires

The concept of modulo arithmetic. Addition, subtraction and multiplication in
given moduli. Application to daily life, such as days of the week, months, and
clock time.

## The idea

Two numbers are congruent modulo n when they leave the same remainder on division
by n. In modulo 5, the number 23 is congruent to 3, because 23 divided by 5 leaves
remainder 3.

## Operations

Work out the ordinary answer, then reduce it modulo n by taking the remainder. In
modulo 7, 5 + 6 = 11, and 11 leaves remainder 4, so the answer is 4.

Negative numbers are reduced by adding multiples of n until the result is between
0 and n - 1. In modulo 6, negative 8 becomes negative 8 + 12 = 4.

## Everyday use

Days of the week are modulo 7 and clock times are modulo 12 or 24. If today is
Tuesday, the day 30 days from now is found by reducing 30 modulo 7, which gives 2,
so it is Thursday.
