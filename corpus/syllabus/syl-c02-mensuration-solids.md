## What the syllabus requires

Surface area and volume of the cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere, prism,
pyramid and the frustum of a cone or pyramid. Composite solids.

## Volumes

Cuboid: length times breadth times height.
Prism: area of the cross-section times the length.
Cylinder: pi r squared h.
Cone: one third pi r squared h.
Pyramid: one third times base area times height.
Sphere: four thirds pi r cubed.

## Surface areas

Cylinder: curved surface is 2 pi r h; total surface of a closed cylinder is
2 pi r h + 2 pi r squared.
Cone: curved surface is pi r l, where l is the slant height, and l squared =
r squared + h squared; total surface of a closed cone is pi r l + pi r squared.
Sphere: 4 pi r squared.

## Frustum

A frustum is what remains when a smaller similar cone or pyramid is cut from the
top of a larger one. Its volume is the volume of the whole solid minus the volume
of the removed top. Work with the two similar solids and the ratio of their
dimensions rather than trying to recall a single formula.

## Similar solids

If two solids are similar with linear scale factor k, their areas are in the ratio
k squared and their volumes are in the ratio k cubed.
