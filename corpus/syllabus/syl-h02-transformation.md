## What the syllabus requires

Reflection of a point or a shape in the x axis, the y axis, and the lines y = x
and y = negative x. Rotation about the origin through 90, 180 and 270 degrees.
Translation by a given vector. Enlargement with a given centre and scale factor.

## Reflection

In the x axis: the point (x, y) maps to (x, negative y).
In the y axis: (x, y) maps to (negative x, y).
In the line y = x: (x, y) maps to (y, x).
In the line y = negative x: (x, y) maps to (negative y, negative x).

## Rotation about the origin

Through 90 degrees anticlockwise: (x, y) maps to (negative y, x).
Through 180 degrees: (x, y) maps to (negative x, negative y).
Through 270 degrees anticlockwise, which is 90 degrees clockwise: (x, y) maps to
(y, negative x).

## Translation

A translation by the vector with components a and b maps (x, y) to (x + a, y + b).

## Enlargement

An enlargement with centre the origin and scale factor k maps (x, y) to (kx, ky).
Lengths are multiplied by k and areas by k squared. A negative scale factor puts
the image on the opposite side of the centre.
