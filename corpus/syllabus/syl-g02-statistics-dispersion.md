## What the syllabus requires

Cumulative frequency tables and curves. Quartiles and percentiles read from the
curve. Range, interquartile range, semi-interquartile range, mean deviation,
variance and standard deviation.

## Cumulative frequency curve

Plot the cumulative frequency against the upper class boundary, not the mid-value,
and join with a smooth curve. This is the ogive.

From the curve: the median is at half the total frequency, the lower quartile at
one quarter, the upper quartile at three quarters. The nth percentile is at n over
100 of the total.

## Measures of spread

Range = largest value minus smallest value.
Interquartile range = upper quartile minus lower quartile.
Semi-interquartile range = half of the interquartile range.

Mean deviation = the sum of f times the modulus of (x - mean), divided by the sum
of f.

Variance = the sum of f times (x - mean) squared, divided by the sum of f.

Standard deviation = the square root of the variance.

## A practical note

Standard deviation has the same units as the data; variance does not. If a question
asks for the standard deviation, remember to take the square root at the end, which
is the step most often forgotten.
