## What the syllabus requires

Angles at a point, on a straight line, and between parallel lines cut by a
transversal. Properties of triangles and quadrilaterals. Angle sums of polygons.
Congruent and similar triangles.

## Basic angle facts

Angles at a point add to 360 degrees. Angles on a straight line add to 180
degrees. Vertically opposite angles are equal.

With parallel lines: corresponding angles are equal, alternate angles are equal,
and co-interior angles add to 180 degrees.

## Triangles

The three angles of a triangle add to 180 degrees. An exterior angle of a triangle
equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles. Base angles of an isosceles
triangle are equal.

## Polygons

For a polygon with n sides:
the sum of the interior angles is (n - 2) times 180 degrees;
the sum of the exterior angles is 360 degrees, for every polygon;
for a regular polygon, each exterior angle is 360 divided by n, and each interior
angle is 180 minus the exterior angle.

## Congruency and similarity

Triangles are congruent under SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, or RHS. Triangles are similar
when their angles are equal; corresponding sides are then in the same ratio.
