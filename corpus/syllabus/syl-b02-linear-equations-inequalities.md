## What the syllabus requires

Solve linear equations in one variable and simultaneous linear equations in two
variables, by substitution, elimination and graphically. Solve one linear and one
quadratic equation simultaneously. Change the subject of a formula. Solve linear
inequalities in one variable and represent the solution on a number line and on a
graph.

## Simultaneous linear equations

Elimination: make the coefficients of one variable equal, then add or subtract.
Substitution: make one variable the subject of one equation and put it into the
other. Graphically: the solution is the point where the two lines cross.

## One linear and one quadratic

Make one variable the subject of the linear equation and substitute into the
quadratic. This gives a quadratic in one variable, which has up to two solutions,
and each value must be put back to find its partner.

## Change of subject

Treat it as solving for the required letter. Collect every term containing that
letter on one side, factorise it out, then divide. When the letter appears inside
a root or a power, undo that operation last.

## Inequalities

The rules are those of equations, with one exception: multiplying or dividing both
sides by a negative number reverses the inequality sign. On a number line an open
circle means the endpoint is not included, a filled circle means it is.
