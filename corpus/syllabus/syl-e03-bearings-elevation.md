## What the syllabus requires

Determine the angle of elevation and the angle of depression. Bearings of one
point from another. Problems on heights and distances, including those needing the
sine or cosine rule.

## Elevation and depression

The angle of elevation of an object is measured upwards from the horizontal at the
observer. The angle of depression is measured downwards from the horizontal. The
angle of depression of B from A equals the angle of elevation of A from B, because
they are alternate angles between parallel horizontal lines.

## Bearings

A bearing is measured from north, clockwise, and written with three figures: 005
degrees, 047 degrees, 312 degrees.

The back bearing is the bearing of the first point from the second. If the forward
bearing is less than 180 degrees, add 180; if it is 180 or more, subtract 180.

## Method for a bearings problem

Draw a clear diagram first, with a north line at every point mentioned. Mark the
angles you know, find the angle inside the triangle, then apply the sine or cosine
rule. Most lost marks in this topic come from the diagram, not the trigonometry.
