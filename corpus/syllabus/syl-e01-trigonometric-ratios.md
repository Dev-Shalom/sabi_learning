## What the syllabus requires

Sine, cosine and tangent of an acute angle. Use of tables and calculators. The
ratios of the special angles 30, 45 and 60 degrees without tables. Trigonometric
ratios of angles between 0 and 360 degrees, and the graphs of sine and cosine.

## The right-angled triangle definitions

For an acute angle in a right-angled triangle: sine is opposite over hypotenuse,
cosine is adjacent over hypotenuse, tangent is opposite over adjacent.

## Special angles

sin 30 = 1/2, cos 30 = root 3 over 2, tan 30 = 1 over root 3.
sin 45 = 1 over root 2, cos 45 = 1 over root 2, tan 45 = 1.
sin 60 = root 3 over 2, cos 60 = 1/2, tan 60 = root 3.

These come from the half of an equilateral triangle and from the isosceles
right-angled triangle, and they are expected without a calculator.

## Angles beyond 90 degrees

Use the quadrant rule. In the first quadrant all three ratios are positive; in the
second only sine; in the third only tangent; in the fourth only cosine. Find the
acute reference angle from the x axis, evaluate the ratio for it, then attach the
sign for the quadrant.

## Identities

sin squared theta + cos squared theta = 1, and tan theta = sin theta over cos
theta.

## Not on this syllabus

Compound angle formulae, the factor formulae, and the differentiation or
integration of trigonometric functions are Further Mathematics.
