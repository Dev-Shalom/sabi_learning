## What the syllabus requires

Perimeter and area of triangles, quadrilaterals, circles, and composite figures.
Length of an arc, area of a sector and area of a segment of a circle. The
Pythagoras relation.

## Standard results

Area of a triangle = half times base times height. Area of a triangle given two
sides and the included angle = half ab sin C. Area of a trapezium = half times the
sum of the parallel sides times the distance between them. Area of a parallelogram
= base times height.

Circumference of a circle = 2 pi r. Area of a circle = pi r squared. In WAEC
questions, take pi as 22 over 7 unless the question states otherwise.

## Arcs, sectors and segments

For a sector of angle theta degrees in a circle of radius r:
length of arc = (theta over 360) times 2 pi r;
area of sector = (theta over 360) times pi r squared.

Area of a segment = area of the sector minus the area of the triangle formed by
the two radii and the chord, that is
(theta over 360) times pi r squared minus half r squared sin theta.

## Pythagoras

In a right-angled triangle the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of the
squares on the other two sides.
