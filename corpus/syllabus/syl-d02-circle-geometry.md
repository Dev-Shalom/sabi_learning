## What the syllabus requires

Chords, arcs, angles in a circle, cyclic quadrilaterals and tangents. Use of the
circle theorems to calculate angles, with reasons.

## The theorems

The angle subtended by an arc at the centre is twice the angle it subtends at any
point on the remaining part of the circumference.

Angles in the same segment are equal.

The angle in a semicircle is a right angle.

Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add to 180 degrees.

The exterior angle of a cyclic quadrilateral equals the interior opposite angle.

A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.

Two tangents drawn from an external point are equal in length.

The alternate segment theorem: the angle between a tangent and a chord equals the
angle in the alternate segment.

A line from the centre perpendicular to a chord bisects the chord.

## In the exam

Every angle you state should be justified by naming the theorem used. Marks in
Paper 2 are given for the reason as well as the value.
