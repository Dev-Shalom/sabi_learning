## What the syllabus requires

Determine the pattern of a sequence. The nth term and the sum of an arithmetic
progression (AP) and of a geometric progression (GP). The sum to infinity of a GP.
Word problems using progressions.

## Arithmetic progression

An AP has a constant common difference d. The nth term is
T(n) = a + (n - 1)d, where a is the first term.

The sum of the first n terms is
S(n) = (n/2) times [2a + (n - 1)d], which can also be written as
S(n) = (n/2) times (a + L), where L is the last term.

## Geometric progression

A GP has a constant common ratio r. The nth term is
T(n) = a times r^(n - 1).

The sum of the first n terms is
S(n) = a times (r^n - 1) divided by (r - 1) when r is greater than 1, or
S(n) = a times (1 - r^n) divided by (1 - r) when r is less than 1. The two forms
are the same expression.

## Sum to infinity

A GP has a sum to infinity only when the common ratio satisfies minus 1 < r < 1.
Then S(infinity) = a divided by (1 - r).

If the modulus of r is 1 or more the series does not converge and there is no sum
to infinity.

## Not on this syllabus

Arithmetic and geometric means beyond the simple insertion of means, convergence
tests, and sigma notation proofs are not required at this level.
