## What the syllabus requires

Simplify surds of the form a root b. Add, subtract, multiply and divide surds.
Rationalise the denominator, including denominators of the form a + b root c.

## Simplifying

Take out the largest perfect square factor. Root 72 is root 36 times root 2, which
is 6 root 2.

## Adding and subtracting

Only like surds can be combined, in the same way as like terms in algebra.
3 root 5 + 7 root 5 = 10 root 5, but root 2 + root 3 cannot be simplified.

## Rationalising a simple denominator

Multiply top and bottom by the surd in the denominator. So 6 over root 3 becomes
6 root 3 over 3, which is 2 root 3.

## Rationalising with a conjugate

For a denominator of the form a + b root c, multiply top and bottom by the
conjugate a - b root c. The denominator becomes a squared minus b squared times c,
which has no surd in it.

For example, 1 over (3 + root 2) is multiplied by (3 - root 2) over (3 - root 2),
giving (3 - root 2) over (9 - 2), that is (3 - root 2) over 7.

## Not on this syllabus

Surds with cube roots or higher roots in a denominator are not required.
