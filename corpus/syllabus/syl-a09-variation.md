## What the syllabus requires

Direct, inverse, joint and partial variation. Application to simple practical
problems, including percentage increase and decrease in varying quantities.

## Direct variation

y varies directly as x means y = kx for some constant k. Doubling x doubles y.

## Inverse variation

y varies inversely as x means y = k divided by x. Doubling x halves y.

## Joint variation

y varies jointly as x and z means y = kxz. y may also vary directly as one
quantity and inversely as another, for example y = kx divided by z.

## Partial variation

A partial variation has two parts, one constant and one varying, for example
y = a + bx, or y = a + b divided by x. Two pairs of values are needed to find both
constants, which gives a pair of simultaneous equations.

## Method

Always write the relation with the constant k first, substitute the given pair of
values to find k, then use the relation with k known to answer the question.
