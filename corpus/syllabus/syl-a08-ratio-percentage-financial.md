## What the syllabus requires

Ratio and rate. Direct and inverse proportion. Percentage increase and decrease.
Profit and loss, simple interest, compound interest, discount, commission, hire
purchase, value added tax, depreciation, and problems on personal and household
finance.

## Ratio and sharing

To share an amount in a given ratio, add the parts to get the total number of
shares, divide the amount by the total, then multiply by each part.

## Percentage change

Percentage change is the change divided by the original value, times 100. Note the
denominator: it is always the original, never the new value. Profit per cent is
profit divided by cost price, times 100.

## Simple interest

I = P times R times T divided by 100, where P is the principal, R is the rate per
cent per annum and T is the time in years. The amount is P + I.

## Compound interest

The amount after n years is A = P times (1 + R/100)^n, and the compound interest
is A - P. Where interest is compounded more than once a year, divide the rate and
multiply the number of periods accordingly.

## Depreciation

Depreciation by a fixed percentage each year uses A = P times (1 - R/100)^n.

## Hire purchase

Hire purchase price is the deposit plus the total of all instalments. The extra
charge is the hire purchase price minus the cash price, and it is expressed as a
percentage of the cash price.

## Value added tax

VAT is added as a percentage of the price before tax. To remove VAT from a
tax-inclusive price, divide by (1 + rate), do not simply subtract the percentage.
