## What the syllabus requires

Vectors as directed line segments. Cartesian components of a vector. Magnitude and
direction. Addition and subtraction of vectors, equal vectors, the zero vector,
multiplication by a scalar, and position vectors.

## Components and magnitude

A vector written as the column with components x and y has magnitude the square
root of (x squared + y squared), by Pythagoras.

Its direction is the angle theta from the positive x axis, where tan theta = y
over x, with the quadrant taken into account.

## Operations

Add and subtract component by component. Multiplying by a scalar k multiplies both
components by k and so multiplies the magnitude by the modulus of k; a negative k
also reverses the direction.

## Position vectors

The position vector of a point A is the vector from the origin to A. The vector AB
equals the position vector of B minus the position vector of A. This subtraction,
in that order, is the single most useful fact in the topic.

## Not on this syllabus

The scalar or dot product, the vector product, and vectors in three dimensions are
Further Mathematics.
