What Sabi stores about you

Most people using Sabi are under 18, so it collects as little as it can get away with. Here is the whole list, in plain words.

You do not have an account

There is no sign-up, no name, no phone number. Your browser holds a random id that Sabi made up — something like 7f3c… with no meaning. That id is how it counts your {{ cap }} questions a day. Clear your browser data and the id is gone and you are a new person to Sabi.

Your questions

The question you type and the answer you get are stored, attached to that random id. They are kept so that when you mark an answer wrong, the mistake can actually be found and fixed. They are not attached to your name, because Sabi does not know it.

Your network address

Sabi stores a scrambled version of your IP address, not the address itself. It cannot be turned back into the original. It exists only to stop one person clearing their browser repeatedly to get unlimited free questions.

Feedback

When you tap Yes or No on an answer, that is stored against the answer and the random id, and nothing else.

Email

Only if you type one into the waitlist yourself. It is never required, and it is used for one thing: telling you if the daily limit changes.

What Sabi never does

No adverts. Nothing sold to anyone. No trackers from other companies. No location. Your exam answers are not shown to your school.

Sabi can be wrong. It is a study aid, not a teacher and not the exam board. For anything that matters, your teacher and the official WAEC syllabus are the authority.
