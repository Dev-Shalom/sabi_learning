Why the codes matter

Sabi cannot send email. There is no address behind it and nothing in this deployment can post a message to you, so there is no reset link and no forgotten-password mail. If you forget your password, one of these codes is the only way back into your account. Without one, the account and everything you have asked stay locked.

Keeping them safe

Anyone holding a code can get into your account, so keep them where a classmate borrowing your phone will not scroll past them. If you use one, your dashboard tells you how many are left.
