What happens when you ask

Sabi has no general knowledge of any subject. It can only use documents it holds, which is what makes the citation worth something.

  1. You ask in your own words

    Type it the way you would say it to a classmate. No special phrasing, and no need to name the topic.

  2. Sabi searches only its own documents

    Syllabus text and worked examples, searched two ways at once — by the words you used and by what you meant — then ranked.

  3. It answers, and every claim carries its source

    A locator like SYL B3 opens the exact passage, with the sentence the answer leans on underlined. If Sabi invents a source that does not exist, the answer is thrown away rather than shown to you.

  4. If it is not sure, it says so

    A wrong answer you trust is worse than no answer. When the documents do not support a confident answer, Sabi tells you, and points you at what it does have.

What Sabi will not do

Worth knowing before you rely on it two weeks before a paper.

Answer from outside its documents If the syllabus material is not there, you get a refusal, not a guess. That includes topics on other syllabuses, like Further Maths.
Quote a real past paper Sabi does not hold licensed WAEC or NECO papers, so it never claims a year or a question number. Worked examples are labelled as worked examples.
Replace your teacher It can be wrong. The reason every answer shows its source is so that you can check it in about five seconds.
Write your essay or your exam It explains method and shows where the method is written down. That is the part that helps you in the hall.

Questions

The ones students and their parents actually ask first.

Is it really free?

Fifteen questions a day without an account, and twenty-five with a free one. There is no card and no trial that quietly ends. The number left today is in the corner of every page, from your first question rather than your fifteenth.

Do I need an account?

No. You can ask straight away. An account raises your daily limit, remembers what you have asked, keeps a notebook of answers you save, and tracks which topics you are weakest on. If you sign up later, the questions you already asked come with you.

Can Sabi be wrong?

Yes, and pretending otherwise would be the most dangerous thing this product could do. What it will not do is hide it: every claim opens to the passage it came from, so checking is quick. When the documents do not support an answer, it refuses instead of guessing.

Will it use up my data?

A page is a few kilobytes of text. The two typefaces together are about 100 KB, downloaded once and then cached. There is no video, no advert and no tracker, and every page works with JavaScript turned off.

Is using this cheating?

It shows you the method and the exact place the method is written down, the way a textbook and a worked-solutions booklet do. It does not sit the exam for you. Ask it to explain a step you did not follow in class, then do the next one yourself.

What do you store about me?

As little as possible, because most people using this are under 18. No name and no phone number. Your address is stored only as a one-way scramble, and only to stop one person using up everyone's free questions. There are no adverts and nothing is sold. The full version, in plain words.
