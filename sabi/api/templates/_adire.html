{# The adire ground. Four resist-dye motifs from Yoruba adire eleko, tiled behind a dark band: bundled parallel lines, concentric rings, a dotted grid, and small crosses. Stroked rather than filled, and deliberately not perfectly regular -- resisted cloth is drawn by hand with a feather, and a machine-exact repeat reads as wallpaper. It inherits `currentColor` from `.adire`, so the theme decides its colour, and it is always behind a solid content surface -- never under body text. #}
