{# A resist-dye edge. Where adire cloth comes out of the indigo vat, the boundary is never a straight line: the paste holds the dye back unevenly and it bleeds a little past the edge, leaving speckles in the undyed ground. This is that boundary, and it is the reason the sections of this page do not simply butt together along a ruled line. It sits at the top of the lower band and is filled with the colour of the band ABOVE it, so the upper ground reads as having run down into this one. The caller sets which colour that is with a `.dye-*` class. Purely decorative, so it is hidden from assistive technology. #}
