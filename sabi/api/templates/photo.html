What happens to the picture

It is read and then discarded. Sabi keeps the text it read and a fingerprint of the file so that sending the same photo twice does not cost you twice — it does not keep the picture, and it cannot get it back. Your handwriting, your name in the margin and your school's stamp are not stored anywhere.
